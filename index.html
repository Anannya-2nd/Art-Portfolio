About Me

Hi, I’m Anannya, though most people know me online as Azuki — a self-taught digital artist driven by passion and imagination. I specialize in creating character illustrations inspired by games like Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, Genshin Impact, and other fantasy worlds.
Art, to me, is more than just drawing — it’s a way to breathe life into imagination and share emotions that words can’t express. Every piece I create reflects my love for storytelling through visuals, where colors, expressions, and atmosphere come together to tell tales beyond the canvas.
I’m constantly exploring new techniques and styles to grow as an artist, and I hope my work inspires others just as much as creating it inspires me.
